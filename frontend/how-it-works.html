Your Journey to Better Health

From setup to sustainable results in three simple steps—no complexity, just results

01

Create Your Comprehensive Health Profile

Tell us about yourself in just 5-7 minutes. We'll ask about your health goals, dietary preferences, allergies, lifestyle, and current challenges. This forms the foundation of your personalized plan.

What We'll Ask You:

  • Health Goals: Weight loss, muscle gain, better energy, disease management, or general wellness
  • Current Metrics: Height, weight, age, activity level, and any existing health conditions
  • Food Preferences: Vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan, regional cuisine preferences (North/South Indian, etc.)
  • Restrictions & Allergies: Lactose intolerance, gluten sensitivity, nut allergies, religious dietary restrictions
  • Lifestyle Factors: Work schedule, stress levels, sleep patterns, exercise routine
  • Budget & Accessibility: Meal budget, cooking time available, access to ingredients

💡 Pro Tip:

Be honest and detailed! The more we know about you, the better we can personalize your plan. Don't worry—all data is encrypted and HIPAA-compliant.

02

Connect Your Health Data Sources

Link your wearables, health apps, and optional lab results for comprehensive 360° personalization. The more data we have, the smarter your plan becomes.

What You Can Connect:

  • Fitness Trackers: Fitbit, Apple Watch, Garmin, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Xiaomi Mi Band
  • Health Apps: Apple Health, Google Fit, Samsung Health—sync all your existing data
  • Smart Scales: Withings, Fitbit Aria—automatic weight and body composition tracking
  • Sleep Trackers: Oura Ring, Fitbit, Apple Watch—monitor sleep quality and recovery
  • Glucose Monitors (Optional): Continuous glucose monitoring devices for blood sugar optimization
  • Lab Results (Optional): Upload DNA test results (23andMe, AncestryDNA) or microbiome analysis

Why Integration Matters:

  • Real-Time Adaptation: Your plan adjusts automatically based on activity, sleep, and stress
  • No Manual Tracking: Steps, calories burned, and workouts sync automatically—no data entry
  • Holistic View: See how nutrition impacts sleep, energy, mood, and performance
  • Better Results: Users with connected devices see 2.3x better outcomes

✓ Optional But Powerful:

Don't have wearables? No problem! You can still get great results. But if you do connect devices, you'll unlock advanced features like activity-adjusted macros and sleep-based meal timing.

03

Get Your Personalized Plan & Start Tracking

Within minutes, receive your AI-generated meal plans, recipes, macro targets, and daily recommendations. Your plan evolves as you progress—it's never static.

What You'll Receive:

  • Weekly Meal Plans: 7 days of breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks tailored to your preferences
  • Indian Recipes: Step-by-step cooking instructions with ingredients you can actually find
  • Daily Macro Targets: Personalized calories, protein, carbs, and fats based on YOUR body and goals
  • Shopping Lists: Organized grocery lists with everything you need for the week
  • Progress Dashboard: Visual tracking of weight, body composition, energy levels, and goal progress
  • AI Insights: Weekly reports showing what's working and what to adjust

How Your Plan Adapts:

  • Daily Adjustments: Macros change based on yesterday's activity and today's schedule
  • Weekly Optimization: AI analyzes your progress and fine-tunes recommendations
  • Plateau Breaking: Automatic strategy shifts when progress stalls (calorie cycling, carb timing, etc.)
  • Preference Learning: The more you log, the better AI understands your taste preferences
  • Seasonal Adaptation: Meal plans adjust for festivals, holidays, and special occasions

Easy Food Logging:

  • Barcode Scan: Instant logging for packaged foods—3 seconds per item
  • Photo Recognition: Snap a picture of your meal—AI identifies and logs it
  • Voice Input: Say what you ate—"I had aloo paratha with curd"—done!
  • Quick Search: Type-ahead search finds Indian foods instantly
  • Meal Templates: Save favorite meals for one-tap logging
